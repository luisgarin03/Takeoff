# Guardrail quantity rules — research for a future phase

This is reference research only. The current Guardrail takeoff row reports the calibrated plan path in LF. It does not calculate bid quantities, terminals, transitions, posts, splices, or stock lengths. Confirm the project's governing specification, special provisions, plan notes, and pay-item schedule before using any future bid-rule calculator.

## TxDOT metal beam guard fence (W-beam and thrie beam)

**Issuer:** Texas Department of Transportation (TxDOT). **Editions reviewed:** 2014 Standard Specifications, Item 540, and 2024 Standard Specifications, Item 540. **System/material scope:** metal beam rail elements under AASHTO M 180; the 2014 material table lists W-beam, thrie beam, and W-beam-to-thrie-beam transition shapes. The 2024 pay language distinguishes Metal W-Beam Guard Fence and Metal Thrie Beam Guard Fence. Both are rail systems supported by posts/blocks and hardware; they are not interchangeable names for one profile.

| Work item | TxDOT measurement unit and boundary | Terminal / transition treatment |
|---|---|---|
| W-beam or thrie-beam guard fence, 2024 Item 540 §4.1 | Foot (LF), measured along the **face of the rail in place**, center-to-center of end splice locations. | The standard rail run excludes separately measured transitions. |
| Rail transitions, 2024 Item 540 §4.2 | Each transition (EA). | Transition is a separate quantity. The payment clause describes nested rail, W-to-thrie transitional rail, posts, curb/concrete, and connections as part of the transition item's compensation. |
| Short-radius fence, 2024 Item 540 §4.3 | Foot, rounded to the nearest whole foot along the rail face from the first CRT post (radius beginning) to the radius end. | Special measurement boundary; not ordinary centerline path footage. |
| Driveway terminal anchor section, 2024 Item 540 §4.4 | Each complete section (EA), consisting of the terminal anchor post and one 6-ft rail section. | Included as its own assembly quantity. |
| Downstream anchor terminal, 2024 Item 540 §4.5 | Each complete section (EA), consisting of one W-beam end section, two downstream anchor posts, and one rail section. | Included as its own assembly quantity. |
| Long-span system, 2024 Item 540 §4.6 | Each complete system (EA), from first CRT post to last CRT post. | Special system measurement, not ordinary rail LF. |
| Older 2014 Item 540 | §4.1 used foot along rail face, center-to-center of end splice locations. §4.2 terminal anchor sections were EA with one 25-ft rail section; §4.3 transitions were EA; §4.4 short radius used nearest whole foot along the rail face; §4.5 driveway anchor sections were EA with 6-ft rail; §4.6 downstream anchor terminal sections were EA; §4.7 long-span system was foot along the face between first and last CRT posts. | Numbering and terminal-anchor rules differ from 2024; project special provisions can amend either edition. |

**Bid-tab example supplied for research:** TxDOT's Project F 2023(819), last updated July 20, 2023, lists item 5406001001 “MTL W-BEAM GD FEN (TIM POST)” in LF (5,152 LF), item 5406006001 “MTL BEAM GD FEN TRANS (THRIE-BEAM)” in EA (12), and item 5446001 “GUARDRAIL END TREATMENT (INSTALL)” in EA (12). This is a bid-schedule example, not a current project rule; TxDOT says that tabulation is no longer updated and directs users to Contract Letting.

## Related systems and why their units should stay distinct

| System / issuer / edition | Measured unit and limits | Terminals / transitions |
|---|---|---|
| TxDOT Item 543, Cable Barrier System, 2024 Standard Specifications | Install, relocate, and remove are each measured by the foot of cable barrier system. | Each cable barrier terminal system is separately measured by each (EA). |
| TxDOT Item 514, Permanent Concrete Traffic Barrier, 2024 Standard Specifications | Foot (LF). Where a barrier has two longitudinal half-sections, measure once along the centerline between the halves. | This measurement paragraph does not create an LF quantity per exposed face or per half-section. Check plan details and other pay items for connections/end treatments. |

These examples show why plan-length and pay quantities need separate fields: standard metal rail uses a face-of-rail splice boundary; concrete barrier uses its centerline; cable barrier distinguishes system LF from terminal EA; and Item 540 treats some rail transitions and terminal assemblies as EA. None of these bid limits changes the visual Guardrail plan-LF row.
